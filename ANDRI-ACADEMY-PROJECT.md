### How to make the narration in ElevenLabs (step by step)

1. In Chrome (Claude in Chrome, your own new tab), go to **https://elevenlabs.io/app/speech-synthesis/text-to-speech**. Bennett is already signed in; never sign in or change account settings.
2. Voice: click the voice row in the Settings panel on the right → My Voices → pick the voice for the language from the table above (English: "UK voice voice to use for ANDRI vids"). Check the voice chip above the text box shows that name.
3. Settings used for 1.1 and 1.2 (keep them the same so all videos sound alike): model **Eleven v4**, Stability 50, Similarity 75, Speed 100, output **MP3 44.1 kHz 128 kbps**. The downloaded file name confirms them (`..._pvc_sp100_s50_sb75_v4.mp3`).
4. Paste the whole script in one go, one narration line per paragraph (blank line between lines), so the voice stays consistent. For a single fix, paste just that line.
5. Click **Generate speech**. It makes two takes (Generation 1 and 2); use Generation 1 unless it sounds wrong.
6. Download with the download arrow at the bottom right of the player bar. The file lands in `~/Downloads` as `ElevenLabs_<date>T<time>_<voice name>_....mp3`. Move it straight into your project's `audio/` folder (check the History tab text matches your script).
7. In the project: split the narration into lines by silence (`ffmpeg ... silencedetect`), trim each line, place them on the timeline with `adelay`, mix with clicks and whooshes, `loudnorm I=-14`, then check about -14 LUFS. See `projects/2026-10-08-academy-1-2-in-depth-or-plan/audio/` for a worked example (`uk/L*.wav`, `mix-uk*.wav`).
8. Past generations are in the History tab on the right if a take needs to be downloaded again.

## Accuracy rule (non-negotiable)

These are educational videos. If anything shown is wrong, the video fails its purpose.

- Nothing may be invented. Every screen, button, label, menu, dialog, file name, flow, answer and animation must match the real Andri app.
- For **every** video, before the story check and storyboard: open Andri staging yourself in Chrome and go through the exact flow the video teaches, step by step. Capture the real screens and real text. Know exactly what everything looks like before animating anything.
- Build the animations from those captures. Animations may only show what really happens in the app, in the order it happens.
- For German, Dutch and French videos, check what the app actually shows in that language (UI language, labels). Do not translate screens yourself if the app shows them differently.
- Before delivery, compare the final contact sheet against the staging captures, screen by screen.
- During production too: go back into staging and re-check each screen before you animate it (see step 5 below). Checking only before the storyboard is not enough.

## Pacing and length

- **Pacing matters more than speed.** The videos should be engaging, but going too fast is not good: every step needs time to land before the next one starts. Calm narration (about 120 words a minute), short pauses between steps, and each screen held long enough to read.
- **Good examples of pacing: 1.1, 1.2 and 2.1** (in `~/Desktop/Andri Videos/Andri Academy/English/`). Match their rhythm.
- **Length depends on the topic.** "Refer to 1.1" means its look, style and pacing, not its length. A video takes as long as its topic needs to be explained properly: a small topic can be about a minute, a bigger one longer. Don't pad and don't rush. (This still doesn't mean 10-minute videos; the target length in the video brief is a guide.)

## Never leave anything to assumption (Bennett, 8 Oct 2026)

- **Main rule:** the viewer only knows what the video shows or says. If a typed request names a client, a document or a fact (e.g. "Priya Nair", "the judgment"), first show where Andri gets it, for example the case file in the matter's Knowledge Base, with a narration line saying so. See "Rule one" in `ACADEMY-LESSONS.md`.

- If something appears on screen that the viewer did not see being set up (a file in the @ list, a template, a memory, a connected mailbox), say in one short narration line where it came from, e.g. "Once your firm's template is in the Knowledge Base, type the at sign and pick it."
- Do not show again what an earlier video already teaches (uploading to the Knowledge Base is 1.1). Mention it in passing instead; a brief highlight on the relevant button (e.g. Knowledge Base) is enough.
- Keep overlap between videos small, but never so small that a step seems to come from nowhere.

## Script

- Calm pace for narration (about 120 words a minute).
- Read the opening out loud and avoid repeating the same key word. In 1.1 "matter" came up too often at the start.
- Translations: write the German, Dutch and French scripts natively (not word for word), and check they still match what is on screen.
  - **Rewrite, don't translate (Bennett, 12 Oct 2026).** Take what each English line means and say it the way a native speaker would, in natural spoken German or Dutch. A word-for-word translation sounds off. It's fine to change sentence order, idioms, or how a line is split, as long as each line still lands on the same step and matches the screen.
  - Read the finished script out loud in the target language: does it sound like a colleague explaining, or like a translation? Rewrite anything that sounds like a translation.
  - Everything else on screen goes into the language too, not just the voice: titles, step captions, the end card, callouts, typed requests, the app's UI (set the staging firm language under Company → Language), and the case documents themselves (German case files: `projects/2026-10-12-academy-1-1-first-matter-de/demo-files-de/`).

## Per video, in order

1. Read the video's brief in `andri-academy-videos.html`.
2. Go through the flow in Andri staging in Chrome; capture every screen.
3. Ask any open questions.
4. Story check → look check → **storyboard, always before any build** (motion-graphics skill), approved by Bennett. Never start animating a video before its storyboard exists. From 1.2 onwards (Bennett, 8 Oct 2026): make the storyboard, then go straight on to build the video without waiting for approval, and continue to the next video in the list the same way. Bennett reviews the finished videos.
5. Build on canvas (reuse the 1.1 project as the template), English UK narration, render with Playwright + FFmpeg.
   **While producing, go back into Andri staging (Bennett, 8 Oct 2026; required):** before animating each beat, open that exact screen in staging again in Chrome and re-check it against what you are about to draw: every label, button, menu, tag, file name, icon, layout, order of steps and what appears when. Click through it yourself so you know how it moves (what opens, what slides in, how long Andri takes). Fix the rebuild before animating, never after. Save any new captures to `staging-captures/`.
6. Contact sheet review at least twice, plus an accuracy check: open staging again and compare the finished contact sheet with the live app, screen by screen, not only with the captures.
7. Then the German version, then the Dutch version, then the French version (see the 80-video process at the top).
