# Rejected video ideas

Every Academy video idea Bennett said no to, and why. The academy video loop
reads this before it proposes anything, so an idea he turned down never comes
back.

**Before proposing a video:** look for the same feature, flow or angle in this
table. If you find it, don't propose it, unless the product has changed since the
date in its row so that the reason no longer holds. In that case, say so in the
proposal and link the frontend PR that changed it.

**When Bennett says no in a storyboard thread:** add a row here with his reason
in his own words, then open a PR. If his reason was a factual mistake in the
storyboard (the app doesn't work that way), also add it to
`ACADEMY-LESSONS.md` so the mistake isn't repeated in another video.
