What the staging check found

Wrong in the current scripts

  • 1.2 Modes: The web app has two modes, not four. The mode button under the chat box offers only In-Depth Mode (default) and Plan Mode. Fast and Outcome don't exist on the web; the iPhone app has Fast and Deep (release notes, July 2026).
  • 4.2 Simulate: The sides are labelled Claimant and Defendant, not "respondent", even in a UK employment matter.
  • 3.2 After hours: Reflections don't send an email every morning. Andri only emails "when there is a genuine insight to share". Don't promise a daily 07:00 email.
  • 9.2 BaseNet: BaseNet isn't in staging. The only firm connector is LegalSense (Dutch practice management, live since 5 Aug 2026). Keep 9.2 on hold, or swap it for LegalSense.
  • 5.1 Review to memo: Turning a finished review table into a client memo could not be confirmed. Leave it out of the video.

In Andri, but in no video

  • Connect files and calendars: The matter Knowledge Base has a Connect button: Microsoft SharePoint, Google Drive, Microsoft OneDrive, plus Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 (calendar and mail). No video shows it. Best fit: one step in 9.1.
  • How Andri answers: Personalisation → Andri's answers: Response style (Formal Legal / Practical), Detail level (Concise / Comprehensive), Format (Flowing / Organised), Thinking display. Best fit: one step in 3.1.
  • Firm Customisations: Company → Company has a Firm Customisations box (house rules and styling for every answer) and a firm Language setting (English, Dutch, French, German). Best fit: 11.2.
  • Automatic top-up: Company → Billing has Automatic top-up: when credits run out, Andri buys a batch (from £20) up to a ceiling you set. Off by default. Best fit: 11.2.
  • LegalSense: Live since 5 Aug 2026: create contacts, clients, matters and submatters in LegalSense from chat. Strong replacement for 9.2 for Dutch firms.

Not needed, or too long

  • 1.2 is now very short: With only In-Depth and Plan, 1.2 is about a minute of content. Either keep it as a 1-minute video, or fold it into 1.1 as its last step (that makes 19 videos).
  • Footnotes in Word: Still not in the Word help article. Keep them out of 6.1.
  • Over 2:30: 10.2 (2:34) and 11.2 (2:36) are over the cap. Trim a few seconds from each.

1.1

Your first matter, start to finish

about 1:40Ready to plan

A new user creates a matter, adds the file, asks a first question, checks the source, points Andri at one document and gets a first letter.

Opens onFirst day in Andri and no idea where to start.

What the video must show, in order
  1. Create the matter
    Matters page → yellow New Matter button (top right). Type the matter name.
  2. Add the file
    Open the matter's Knowledge Base (yellow button top right, shows the document count) → Upload Matter Documents. Mention the other way in: Email Documents shows the address to forward files to.
  3. Andri reads every file
    Each file gets a one-line Summary, a Status (Complete) and a Tag (e.g. Letter, Memo). Nair has PDFs, Word, Excel, emails (.eml), WhatsApp screenshots (.jpg) and audio (.mp3): show that range.
  4. Ask a first question
    Back in the matter's chat, the Ask Andri... box. Click Example questions: a panel "Standard Questions: General case analysis questions", written for this matter, with arrows to switch question types. Pick one.
  5. Andri shows its work
    While answering, the steps appear ("Search case documents", "Thinking"), then the answer with source links inline (e.g. "ET3", "judgment of 19 March 2026").
  6. Click a source
    Click a source link: the document opens beside the chat.
  7. One document: @
    Click the @ button (or type @): the list opens on Matter Documents; "Next: Company Documents" switches lists. Pick one document, then ask the question.
  8. A first letter
    Ask for a short letter. It's written in the background; a Document Ready notification lands in Inbox; open it from Jobs in the matter's left sidebar.
Example from the mock case
  • Matter name for step 1: Nair v Calderdale Logistics (then switch to the full one in staging).
  • Files to upload: the employment folder: PDFs, Word, Excel, emails, images and the .mp3 show the range Andri reads.
  • First question: "What does the claimant say about the redundancy process?"
  • Source to click: 03_ET3_Response.pdf or the judgment of 19 March 2026.
  • @ example: @03_ET3_Response then "List the respondent's defences." (Expected: genuine redundancy, fair consultation, Thornton's role different, no discrimination, failure to mitigate.)
  • First letter: "Draft a short update letter to Priya Nair on where her claim stands."
Worth saying or showing

  • Under every answer: thumbs up/down, copy, and Edit In Drafts.
  • Footer under the chat box: "Andri is an AI system and can make mistakes, so please double-check responses."
