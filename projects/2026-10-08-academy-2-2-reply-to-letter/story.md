## What staging showed that the brief got wrong or left out

- "Always remember: we write to the other side in formal English." saves a **Personal** memory, not a firm one. Only "Remember for the whole firm: …" saves a firm memory, and Andri asks you to confirm first (Yes, go ahead / No). The video uses the firm wording.
- Pointing at the earlier settlement letter makes Andri ask how to mention it, because it was "without prejudice". The recommended answer keeps the £52,000 figure out of the reply. That is the "Andri asks first" moment, and it is a real, useful one.
- Andri also asked how to date the reply (the letter is dated April, staging is October). The video skips that first question because it only exists because of the demo dates.
- The finished document opens on its own beside the chat when it's ready, and it is also in Jobs.
