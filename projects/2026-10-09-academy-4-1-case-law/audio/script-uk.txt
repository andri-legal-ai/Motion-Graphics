Case law research usually means a dozen open tabs. Here's how to do it all in Andri.

Open Explore, then Case law. The newest rulings come first, from the UK, the Netherlands, Belgium and Switzerland.

Narrow the list on the left by legal area, decision date or court. Here, employment law.

Each ruling is summarised for you: the court, the citation and the outcome, then the key holdings, the legal issues and the cases it cites.

To find something specific, search by keyword. Matching words are highlighted in every summary.

View case opens the full judgment on Bailey, so you can check it at the source.

To compare rulings, tick the ones you need, choose Add to tabular review, and pick the matter.

Each ruling becomes a row. Ask your question once, start the review, and Andri answers it for every ruling, with its sources.

Or just ask in the matter's chat. Andri searches case law and legislation itself, and cites the authorities it relies on.

Find it, read it, compare it, or just ask. That's case law research in Andri.
