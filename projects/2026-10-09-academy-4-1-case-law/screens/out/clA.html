The King v Frederic Priestley
The King / Frederic Priestley
Criminal law
8 Oct 2026
|
Appeal Against Sentence
|
Judge
Andrews Lj
Outcome
Sentence reduced from 35 to 24 months' imprisonment; suspension refused. Remote judge attendance criticised.
The defendant pleaded guilty to fraud by false representation after running a fake flat rental scam against 34 victims, and was sentenced to 35 months' imprisonment by a Recorder who appeared by video link without notice to counsel. The Court of Appeal quashed the sentence and substituted 24 months' imprisonment, holding that the harm categorisation and mitigation were wrongly assessed, but refused to suspend the sentence.
Key Holdings
-
Live link attendance by a judge at a sentencing hearing is exceptional and requires prior consultation with the Resident Judge and advance notice to counsel
-
A statutory direction under section 51 of the Criminal Justice Act 2003 requires the interests of justice and an opportunity for parties to make representations
