Bacon v Fetch! Retail Limited
Claire Bacon / Fetch! Retail Limited
Employment law
1 Jan 2025
|
Employment Law | Maternity Discrimination | Unfair Dismissal
|
Judge
Employment Judge Ayre
Counsel
Christopher Bacon (claimant, Husband)
William Haines (respondent, Litigation Consultant)
Claimant employed as Senior Category Manager from April 2022 to March 2024 was dismissed following redundancy. Claimant claimed automatic unfair dismissal on grounds of maternity leave, pregnancy and maternity discrimination, and detriment. Tribunal found redundancy was genuine reason for dismissal; male colleague in same role also made redundant; delay in consultation protected claimant's employment; cancelled KIT days were due to business pressures not maternity status; no suitable alternative roles available. All claims dismissed.

Ballerino v The Racecourse Association Limited
Miss Helen Ballerino / The Racecourse Association Limited
Employment law

|

|
Judges
Mrs Justice Eady Dbe (president), Ms Kashmir Bilgan, Miss Natalie Swift
Counsel
Robin Pickard (instructed By Free Representation Unit) For Appellant
Safia Tharoo (instructed By Gateley Legal) For Respondent
Claimant dismissed during maternity leave, purportedly by reason of redundancy when her Financial Accountant role was subsumed into new Finance Manager and Business Analyst position. Claimant alleged sham redundancy and sex/maternity discrimination. ET dismissed claims. EAT allowed appeal, holding ET failed to address whether genuine redundancy existed under section 139 ERA, which was essential to determining both unfair dismissal and discrimination claims.
