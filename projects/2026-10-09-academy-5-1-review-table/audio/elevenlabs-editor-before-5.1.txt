Hearing on Thursday, and you still don't know the other side's best argument? Prepare it, and rehearse it, with Andri.

In your matter, open the Timeline. It's the first icon on the left.

Andri builds it from every file in the Knowledge Base: an overview of the case, the disputed issues, the parties, and where things stand.

Every fact is linked to its source. Click one, and the document opens beside your chat.

Close it, and open the Timeline again. Below the summary is every key date, in order.

Something the file doesn't show yet, like a call with your client? Add it yourself: a title, a date, then save.

Now ask Andri what each document proves.

It reads every file, then sets out what each document proves, and its limits. It also flags what to fix first, like a schedule of loss that doesn't add up.

Then ask for your hearing notes. If something is unclear, Andri asks first: here, which hearing, and where you want the notes.

The notes arrive in the chat, built on what it just found.

Now rehearse. Open Simulate: a judge, counsel for the Claimant, and counsel for the Defendant, over six turns.

Start the simulation. The judge reads the case file and the law, fixes the issues and who has to prove each one, then opens the hearing.

Next Step runs the next turn: counsel for the Claimant, then the Defendant. Each turn shows what it searched.

To start again, use the circular arrow.

Know your file, then hear the other side before the judge does.
