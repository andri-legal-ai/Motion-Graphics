CLEANING SERVICES CONTRACT

Dated 1 November 2024

Parties

(1) Ashby Facilities Limited, a company registered under number 06645678, whose registered office is at 3 Kirkgate, Huddersfield HD1 1QS (the Supplier); and

(2) Pennbridge Foods Limited, a company registered in England and Wales under number 05512340, whose registered office is at Pennbridge House, Flanshaw Lane, Wakefield WF2 9JF (the Customer).

1. Supply

1.1 The Supplier shall provide to the Customer hygiene cleaning of the Customer's production areas at its Ossett site, as described in Schedule 1 (the Services) in accordance with this Agreement.

1.2 The Supplier shall perform its obligations with reasonable skill and care, in accordance with good industry practice and all applicable laws, including food safety and hygiene legislation.

2. Term

2.1 This Agreement starts on 1 November 2024 and continues for three years, ending on 31 October 2027, unless terminated earlier under clause 6.

3. Price and payment

3.1 The Customer shall pay the charges set out in Schedule 2. The Supplier shall invoice monthly in arrears and the Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within 30 days of receipt.

3.2 Charges are exclusive of VAT, which the Customer shall pay at the applicable rate.

4. Confidentiality

4.1 Each party shall keep the other's confidential information confidential and use it only to perform this Agreement, during its term and for three years after it ends.

5. Liability

5.1 Nothing in this Agreement limits liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, or for fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation.

5.2 The Supplier's total liability arising under or in connection with this Contract shall not exceed £100,000 in aggregate.

5.3 The Supplier shall maintain public and product liability insurance with a reputable insurer throughout the term.

6. Termination

6.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience by giving the other not less than 60 days written notice.

6.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other commits a material breach which is not remedied within 30 days of being notified of it, or becomes insolvent.

7. General

7.1 This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties. No variation is effective unless in writing and signed by both parties.

7.2 This Agreement and any dispute arising from it are governed by the law of England and Wales, and the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.
