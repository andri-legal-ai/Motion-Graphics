Five versions of one letter, and their clauses changed by hand? Finish both in Word, with Andri.

Andri works inside Word on Windows, Mac and the web. You need Microsoft 365 and an Andri Business plan.

To add it, open Add-ins on the Home tab, then More Add-ins, and search for Andri.

Select Andri and choose Add. If your firm has already added it for you, you'll see Open instead.

Open Andri from your add-ins, and sign in with the Andri account you already use.

The pane shows your matters. Search them, sort them, or start a new one. Only matters you own appear here.

Open a matter. Its Word files are under Case files.

Here is our update letter to Ms Nair, already in the matter's Knowledge Base. Open it, and it loads straight into Word.

Small fixes, you simply type. Here, today's date.

For a bigger change, select the paragraph. It appears in the pane as your selection. Then tell Andri what you want.

Andri reads the whole letter, rewrites the paragraph, and explains what it changed.

Nothing changes until you review it. For each edit, reject it, accept it, or keep it as a tracked change.

Kept edits arrive as tracked changes, under your own name in Word. Andri turns tracking on for its edit, then puts your setting back.

Click Save to Andri. The letter is saved as a draft on the matter, with a new version every time.

In Andri on the web, you'll find it under Drafts, on the Word tab.

Their contract works the same way, in its own conversation. Open the settlement agreement from the other side's solicitors. Select the confidentiality clause, and give your position.

Andri redrafts the clause, and flags what else to look at, like the one-sided repayment clause.

Keep it tracked, and your redline is ready to send back.

Each question you send uses your firm's credits. Opening matters and saving versions cost nothing.

Your draft and their contract, finished in Word.
