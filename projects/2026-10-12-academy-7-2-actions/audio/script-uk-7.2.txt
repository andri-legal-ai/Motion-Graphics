Typing the same detailed brief into every matter? Write it once as an Action, and run it anywhere.

Open Explore, then Actions.

Actions sit in two lists: Personal, just for you, and Company, for your whole firm. To make one, click Create Action.

First, give it a short name. Then describe the task the way you'd brief a junior colleague.

It needs at least a hundred characters. Then click Next.

Next, you can add up to five documents from your firm's Knowledge Base, like a model or a checklist. This check doesn't need any.

Then, up to three examples of the finished result. Leave it empty, and the action answers in the chat.

Click Create Action. Andri builds it in the background, which takes a few minutes. You can keep working meanwhile.

When it's ready, open it. Your brief is now a skill: the inputs it needs, and a checklist Andri follows every time.

New actions are personal. To give one to your whole firm, click share, then Share with company.

Now open a matter. This one already has the judgment and the schedule of loss in its Knowledge Base.

In the chat, type the at sign, and press the right arrow until you reach Actions. Pick Remedy check, and send it.

Andri applies your brief to this matter's files: each head of loss, whether the judgment supports it, the other side's argument, and the risk.

Actions work in workflows too. Add a Run Action step, and pick one from your library.

Brief Andri once, and run it in any matter.
