Remedy Check: Priya Nair v Calderdale Logistics Limited (1803442/2025)

1. Case Summary

  • Tribunal and judgment date: Employment Tribunal, Leeds, Employment Judge Hartley sitting with members. Reserved judgment sent to the parties on 19 March 2026.
  • Claim presented: 2 June 2025, after Acas early conciliation. The Eighth Addendum Vento bands therefore apply.
  • EDT: 31 March 2025. The judgment makes no finding on it. See check 2 in section 4 on a possible extension to 2 May 2025.
  • Acting for: Claimant
  • Claims succeeded: unfair dismissal under s.98 ERA 1996, automatic unfairness under s.99 ERA 1996, direct sex discrimination under s.13 EqA 2010, and pregnancy and maternity discrimination under s.18 EqA 2010 05_Tribunal_Judgment.
  • Claims failed, withdrawn or struck out: none recorded in the judgment.

2. Findings Register

TopicFindingPara
Credibility and respondent's evidenceThe Claimant was credible and consistent. Mr Marsden's evidence on the restructuring was vague and unsupported by contemporaneous documents.2
Business caseThe claimed 22% fall in volumes was not borne out by the figures, which showed volumes broadly flat with two new clients onboarded in early 2025.2
Redundancy situationMr Thornton was retained in a role materially identical to the Claimant's. No genuine redundancy situation existed.3
Procedure and predeterminationThe consultation was a sham and the decision to dismiss was taken before it began. The s.136 burden shifted and the respondent did not discharge it.4
Discriminatory actsThe dismissal and the reduction in the Claimant's role were direct sex discrimination and pregnancy and maternity discrimination.5
Remedy directionsRemedy to be determined at a further hearing if not agreed. The provisional view is an award for injury to feelings in the middle Vento band, with basic and compensatory awards reflecting loss of earnings, subject to mitigation.6
