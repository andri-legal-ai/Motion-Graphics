Still saving emails and attachments into the file by hand? Here are three ways to bring them straight into Andri.

First, every matter has its own email address. Open Matter Email from the sidebar, and copy the address.

Only addresses on this list can send to it, and yours is there from the start. Add your client, Priya Nair, and her emails come in too. Anyone else gets a polite refusal.

Watch how it works shows what happens next. Forward an email from your own mailbox to the matter's address. Here, Priya's email about her return to work.

Andri saves the email as a document in the matter, and each attachment as a document of its own. Each arrival also shows in your Inbox.

On the Knowledge Base, Email Documents gives you the same address.

Second, files from elsewhere. Connect links SharePoint, Google Drive or OneDrive, and Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 for calendar and mail.

Third, your own mailbox. In Personalisation, under Connectors, sign in to Gmail, Outlook or SharePoint. It stays with you, not the matter, and each new matter starts with it switched on.

Once a mailbox is connected, the Email tab in your Inbox shows your mail. Andri files each email in the right matter, and flags what needs you.

The matter's address, connected files, and your own mailbox. Nothing uploaded by hand.
