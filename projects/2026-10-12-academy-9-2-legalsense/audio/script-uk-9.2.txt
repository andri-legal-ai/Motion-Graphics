Does your firm run LegalSense, the Dutch practice management system? Link it to Andri once, and work with it straight from the chat.

An administrator sets it up once, for the whole firm. Open Company from the top bar, then Company connectors.

LegalSense is listed here, not connected yet. Click Connect.

Andri first shows what it brings: your matters, contacts and time, together in the conversation you're already having.

Once it's linked, you simply ask. Andri finds matters, clients and contacts, registers time, looks up invoices, budgets and third-party funds, and opens new clients and matters.

The administrator enters the firm's installation once. Then each lawyer signs in with their own LegalSense account, so Andri never has more rights than they do.

Click Continue, and enter your firm's LegalSense address. Connecting a system for the whole firm needs two-factor authentication on your own account, so set that up first. Then save the installation.

After that, each lawyer opens Personalisation, then Connectors. LegalSense appears here once it's switched on, and you always sign in with your own login.

Set up once for the firm, one login per lawyer, and LegalSense right in the chat.
