# Style guide from Bennett's reference (13 Oct 2026)

Reference: https://prompt-motion.com/twoclipping-6dd14e ("UGC ad generator promo", @twoclipping), 20 s, 1920 x 1080, 60 fps.
Frames: `frames/` (4 fps), close-ups in `hi/` (open, morph, roll, logo). We take its grammar, never its content or colours.

Bennett: this video may look a little different from the Academy (intro, outro and 2 or 3 big text moments), but it must
still inform like an Academy video. No standing title frame.

| What the reference does | How we use it (Andri brand) |
|---|---|
| Light cream background with a soft warm blob | Andri cream to gold background (unchanged) |
| One heavy sans, tight tracking, lowercase, no full stops | Rethink Sans 700, letter-spacing about -3%, sentence case Dutch, no full stops |
| Words rise one at a time through a mask at their baseline, about 0.12 s apart, slight vertical blur while moving | Same: each word clipped to its line box, rises 0.9 em with a short ghost trail, 0.1 to 0.15 s apart, timed to the voice |
| One accent word in the one accent colour | The accent word gets the Andri yellow highlight (#F7E17E) sweeping in behind it, ink text stays readable on cream |
| The accent word turns into a solid block, the rest blurs away, the block grows into the product window (match cut) | The yellow block grows into the Andri window, which then shows the real app (intro, and back into the app after a text moment) |
| Text changes by rolling up with ghost trails while the next rolls in, small grey subline under it | Roll for the security facts and the webinar date |
| Logo: icon pops, wordmark wipes in from a blur, tagline fades in; out: dims | Outro: Andri icon pops, wordmark wipes in, the BaseNet mark joins it; then fade |
| Banned: shockwaves, particles, RGB split, shake, flares, neon, bouncy easing | Same, plus the house bans (andri-style.md) |

Use: intro (logos and the hook), "vragen stellen over uw dossier" (into the chat), the security facts, the outro (webinar,
academy, logo). Everything else is the Academy walkthrough: real staging plates, cursor, caption pill, rings.
