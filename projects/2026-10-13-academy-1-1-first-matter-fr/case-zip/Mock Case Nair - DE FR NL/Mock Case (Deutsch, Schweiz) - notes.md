**Legal changes (English law → Swiss law)**
- Unfair dismissal / Equality Act → discriminatory dismissal (Art. 3, 5, 6 GlG) and, in the alternative, abusive dismissal (Art. 336 para. 1 lit. a OR). Compensation capped at 6 months' pay (Art. 5 para. 4 GlG, Art. 336a OR) + Genugtuung (Art. 49 OR).
- Acas early conciliation → written objection before the notice ends (Art. 336b para. 1 OR, 14 Apr 2025) and mandatory conciliation (Art. 197, 200 para. 2 ZPO); claim within 180 days (Art. 336b para. 2 OR).
- Notice: 2 months to month end (Art. 335c OR) → employment ends 31 May 2025 (was 31 Mar), with garden leave.
- Maternity leave: 14 weeks (Art. 329f OR) + agreed unpaid leave until 12 Jan 2025, so the story stays the same.
- Barrister (Hester Ramsbottom) removed: in Switzerland the lawyer argues the case.
- Audio: recording a private conversation without consent is a crime in Switzerland (Art. 179ter StGB), so the meeting now opens with "we are recording this for the minutes" (consent).
- Firms: Aldridge Vine Rechtsanwälte AG (Zurich; logo without "LLP"), Calderdale Logistik AG (Dietikon), Bowater Sykes Rechtsanwälte AG. Bank, doctor and all numbers (UID, IBAN) are fictional or official dummy values.
