**Legal changes (English law → Dutch law)**
- Redundancy dismissal needs UWV permission (art. 7:669 lid 3 sub a, 7:671a BW): applied 18 Feb, granted 12 Mar, notice 14 Mar 2025 to 31 May 2025 (2 months, art. 7:672 BW). Dates stay aligned with the English case.
- Discrimination: art. 7:646 BW (no distinction between men and women at dismissal) + art. 1 lid 3 Awgb (pregnancy, childbirth and motherhood = direct sex discrimination); burden of proof art. 7:646 lid 12 BW.
- Remedy: billijke vergoeding (art. 7:681 lid 1 sub c, subsidiary 7:682 lid 1 sub b BW) EUR 45,000 + immaterial damages EUR 7,500 (art. 6:106 BW); transitievergoeding EUR 13,991.18 already paid (art. 7:673 BW). Request filed within 2 months (art. 7:686a lid 4 sub a BW).
- Leave: zwangerschaps- en bevallingsverlof (16 weeks) + unpaid ouderschapsverlof until 12 Jan 2025. The 7:670 lid 2 BW dismissal ban does not apply (dismissal > 6 weeks after return).
- No barrister: the firm's own advocaat pleads. Single kantonrechter (no lay members).
- Firms: Aldridge Vine Advocaten (Amsterdam), Calderdale Logistiek B.V., Bowater Sykes Advocaten. Bank (Grachtenbank N.V.), doctor and numbers are fictional; IBANs are standard example values.
- Audio: Dutch Mac voices (Xander for the manager; Ellen has a Flemish accent).
