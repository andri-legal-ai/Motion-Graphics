RESERVED JUDGMENT
Claims and Issues

1. The Claimant brings complaints of unfair dismissal under the Employment Rights Act 1996 and of direct sex discrimination and pregnancy and maternity discrimination under sections 13 and 18 of the Equality Act 2010. The Respondent says the Claimant was fairly dismissed by reason of redundancy.

Findings of Fact

2. We heard from the Claimant and from Mr Marsden and Ms Caldwell (HR) for the Respondent. We found the Claimant a credible and consistent witness. We found Mr Marsden’s evidence on the alleged restructuring vague and unsupported by contemporaneous documents. The claimed 22% fall in volumes was not borne out by the figures, which showed volumes broadly flat with two new clients onboarded in early 2025.

3. We find that Mr Thornton was recruited to cover the Claimant’s maternity leave and was retained in a role materially identical to the Claimant’s. The distinction drawn by the Respondent between ‘Operations Manager’ and ‘Transport Planning’ was a label without substance. No genuine redundancy situation existed.
