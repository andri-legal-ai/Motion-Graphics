When a client emails you a document, someone has to read it, check it and reply. Here's how to build a workflow that does it for you.

Open Explore, then Workflows, and click New Workflow.

Every workflow starts with a trigger. Choose When an email arrives.

It fires when an email reaches the matter's own inbox, or a connected Gmail or Outlook, and it passes on the sender, the text and every attachment.

Next, the check. From the list, add Run Action, drag it under the trigger and join the two.

Pick the action that holds your checklist. This firm made Remedy check once, under Explore, Actions. The email's attachments go to it automatically.

Then Create Document. Say what the memo should cover, and pick your firm's memo template, so it comes out in your house style.

Nothing should go out unchecked. Add a Pause and Approval step, choose a specific person, Adam Frith here, and write a short note for the request.

Last, Send Email, joined to the Approved side. It goes from your own Outlook or Gmail to whoever sent the email, with the memo attached.

Name the workflow and click Publish. A published workflow is a template: it doesn't run on its own.

To switch it on, open the matter, go to Workflows and click Activate. Add a filter, so only Ms Nair's emails start it.

Andri replies from your own mailbox, so connect your Outlook to this matter with sending turned on. Then click Activate.

From then on, every document Ms Nair sends is checked, written up and answered, as soon as Adam approves.
