Writing the same Monday memo every week? Build it once as a workflow, and Andri writes it for you.

Open Explore, then Workflows, and click New Workflow.

Every workflow starts with a trigger. It can start when an email arrives, when you click Run, on a <phoneme alphabet="ipa" ph="ˈskɛdjuːl">schedule</phoneme>, or before a deadline. For a weekly memo, choose On a <phoneme alphabet="ipa" ph="ˈskɛdjuːl">schedule</phoneme>.

Click the trigger, and set How often to Every week. Monday at nine is filled in for you. It runs on its own, with everything in the matter to hand.

Next, add Run Prompt, place it under the trigger and join the two. Then tell Andri what to do each week: summarise the new documents and emails, and list what needs action.

Add Create Document, and join it on. It turns that summary into a one-page Word memo for the partner.

Name the workflow and click Publish. A published workflow is a template: it doesn't run on its own.

So open the matter's workflows and click Activate. From now on, the memo is written every Monday morning, and each run shows up under Executions.

Workflows can start in other ways, too. Deadline Approaching fires a set number of days before a date you pick in the matter, for example two weeks before a hearing, to start the preparation.

Assignment Updated fires when a task on the matter changes, say to email the partner once it's marked done.

And Manual Trigger runs when you click Run in the matter. Email triggers have their own video.

Build it once, switch it on, and the routine work starts by itself.
