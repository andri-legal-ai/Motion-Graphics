Format: how-to walkthrough for admins, same look as 1.1, 1.2 and 8.1 (centred app window, cream to gold gradient, one caption pill per step, Aldridge Vine demo firm). No welcome intro. About 1:40.

Source: `andri-academy-videos.html` → `#v11-1` (Chapter 11, For admins, status "Fix before filming"). Next video: 11.2 "Set up Andri for your firm".

Brief, in order: Invite (Company → Members → Invite Member: email and role) · The roles (Admin, Member, Support) · Support seats (Company → Billing: "Support-role members are not billed as a seat. Their AI work is paid from the support wallet instead." Credits never expire and are shared across the firm) · The 30-day rule (switching to or from Support is fixed for 30 days) · Someone leaves (transfer their matters first, then remove them). Example: Rosalind Keane as Support (trainee), Adam Frith as Admin. Worth showing: two wallets, Premium (£50 = 8,500 credits, £200 = 34,000, £500 = 85,000) and Support.

## Decisions (answered myself, per ANDRI-ACADEMY-PROJECT.md "Questions")

- Staging has one member, so the role-lock dialog and Remove Member can't appear. They are said in the narration with the app's own wording, over the real screens that lead to them (Role field; Members page). No screen is invented for them.
- No invitation is sent (it would email someone outside Andri). The email is typed in the rebuild only; the Send button is named, then the dialog is closed.
- Adam Frith is the signed-in admin on screen (demo user), so the brief's "invite Adam Frith as Admin" is left out; Rosalind Keane is invited as Support.
- Transfer ownership is opened on my own matter "Academy 11.1" (shown as "Nair v Calderdale Logistics"); nothing is transferred. The staging-only hint "No other active members are available in your firm." is left out (a firm with colleagues doesn't show it).
- The invite dialog's billable-seat note stays as staging shows it (it still shows with Support picked; already reported in 8.1).
- Billing has its own 3-slide explainer ("How does this work?": Support credits · Reading is free, AI work uses credits · Bring the whole team in). Not used: the page's own "How it works" lines say the same and keep the video short. Worth a look for a later edit.
- The "every billable member unlocks one Support seat" line comes from staging's own dialog text ("Support seats unlock at a 1:1 ratio..."), which only shows when a firm runs out of Support seats; it is said over the Support tab.
- Matters list shows only the real Nair row (22 Sept, tag 2) from a staging search; my matter's menu and dialog are placed on it. "Page 1 of 2" reads "Page 1 of 1" with one matter.
- Billing scrolls to where both credit tabs can scroll (staging can't scroll as far on the shorter Support tab), so the tab switch doesn't jump.
