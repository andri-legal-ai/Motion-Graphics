New admin? Here's how to set up your whole firm in one sitting.

Everything starts on the Company page. Click your firm's name in the top bar.

On the Company tab, set the firm's name and its language.

Firm Customisations holds the rules for every answer, such as who you are and how you write.

Type them in, then save with Update Company.

Next, the Knowledge base. Upload your letterhead and your models here, and sort them into folders.

Once they're processed, Andri uses them as context across the firm.

Then invite your team from Members, and give each person the right role.

Saved prompts is the firm's shared library for repeatable tasks, like standard questions. Anyone can save one from a chat.

Firm memories are rules Andri keeps for everyone. Open Personalisation, then Memories. Anything under Firm applies to the whole firm.

Next, a first specialist, under Explore, Specialists. Build it from your models, and Andri consults it whenever a matter needs what it knows.

Then set up one workflow, like a weekly memo, under Explore, Workflows.

Once a month, check Usage. Pick a billing cycle to see the credits used so far, against your allowance.

Last, Billing. Switch on Automatic top-up. When your monthly credits run out, Andri buys a small batch and carries on, up to a ceiling you set.

Or buy credits by hand, here under Credits.

Name, knowledge, people, prompts, memory, a specialist and a workflow. Your firm is ready.
