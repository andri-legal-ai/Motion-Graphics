## Draft script (to check against staging before generating)

1. New admin? Here's how to set up your whole firm in one sitting.
2. Everything starts on the Company page. Click your firm's name in the top bar.
3. On the Company tab, set the firm's name and language. Firm Customisations holds custom rules and styling for every response.
4. Then the Knowledge base. Upload your letterhead and models, and sort them into folders.
5. Invite your team from Members, with the right role for each person.
6. Firm memories hold rules for everyone. You'll find them in Personalisation, under Memories.
7. Build a first specialist from your models, under Explore, Specialists.
8. Saved prompts are a shared library for repeatable tasks. Save one from any chat with the bookmark icon.
9. Set up one workflow, like a weekly memo, under Explore, Workflows.
10. Once a month, check Usage, by billing cycle.
11. And on Billing, switch on Automatic top-up. When credits run out, Andri buys a small batch, up to a ceiling you set. Or buy credits by hand.
12. (end card) Name, knowledge, people, memory, a specialist, prompts and a workflow. Your firm is ready.
